# Brand Voice

Use these defaults unless the source has a clear author voice or the user provides an override.

## Default voice

- Clear, professional, and approachable
- Technical when needed, with unfamiliar ideas explained plainly
- Practical and specific
- Confident without exaggeration or manufactured anxiety
- Personal only when the source supports a personal perspective

Do not use clickbait such as "shocking", "must-read", "the strongest ever", or "you won't believe". Avoid generic claims, forced enthusiasm, repeated rhetorical questions, heavy emoji use, and hashtag padding.

Never invent first-hand experience. Phrases such as "I tested", "we found", or "in our project" require direct support from the source.

## Override hierarchy

User instructions take precedence over these style defaults when they do not conflict with source fidelity. Supported requests include:

- `professional`, `casual`, `personal`, `educational`, `concise`, `technical`, `friendly`
- `no emoji`, `no hashtag`
- `more personal`, `more technical`, `shorter`, `more conversational`
- Platform-specific audience, length, CTA, structure, and link requirements

Apply a global override to every selected platform. Apply a platform-specific override only to that platform.

## Human-writing check

Replace filler with a source detail. Vary sentence length and paragraph endings. Use active voice and concrete nouns. Remove phrases that announce the writing process, praise the article without evidence, or could fit any topic.

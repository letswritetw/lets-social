# Facebook

Use this reference only when generating or revising Facebook content.

## Native goal

Give readers enough context to understand why the author wrote the article and why it matters. Preserve a personal point of view when the source contains one.

## Decisions

1. Choose a structure that fits the source, such as problem to reflection to solution, or story to discovery to share.
2. Include more background than Instagram or Threads when that context helps.
3. Explain the author's motivation only when the source states or clearly demonstrates it.
4. Place the article URL directly in the post when one is available and that matches the user's request.
5. Close with a source-specific CTA. A question works only when it invites a real discussion.

The post may be longer than the other platform versions. Do not turn it into a corporate announcement, press release, or full article summary. Emoji are optional and restrained.

## Default fields

- `Post`
- `CTA`

## Pre-flight

- The post supplies useful context without retelling the whole article.
- The author's perspective has not been invented or flattened.
- The tone sounds like a person sharing useful work.
- Any URL matches the supplied source.
